About JPEG to SVG

This website provides tools to convert JPEG images into SVG files. You can choose an embedded SVG, which keeps the original raster image, or vector tracing, which approximates the image with editable paths.

Conversion results depend on the image and selected method. Review your SVG before using it in important projects.
